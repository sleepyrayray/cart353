# CART 353 Final Project Plan — Miniverse

For my final project, I plan to create **Miniverse**, a 2D generative universe simulation. The screen will begin almost completely empty, and the user will click anywhere to trigger a miniature Big Bang. Particles will spread outward and gradually form stars, planets, moons, and small planetary systems.

The user will mainly observe and explore the universe rather than build it themselves. They will be able to zoom in and out and pan around a space larger than the browser window. The simulation will use simplified gravity, movement, and formation rules so that each universe develops differently. The goal is believable and interesting behaviour, not perfect scientific accuracy.

The project will be made with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and p5.js. It will have a dark, colourful, glowing visual style, with simple shapes that make stars, planets, moons, and particles easy to recognize.

The main focus is the Big Bang, expansion, celestial formation, orbital movement, exploration, and emergence. If there is enough time, I may add collisions, debris, stellar lifecycles, supernovae, black holes, or a Big Crunch that resets the universe.
